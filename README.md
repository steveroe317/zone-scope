# ZoneScope

> ⚠️ **Experimental.** ZoneScope is a personal, in-development app and a coding
> experiment — it is **largely generated by [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code)**.
> Expect rough edges; it is not App Store–ready.

ZoneScope is a small iOS app that reads your workouts and heart-rate data from
Apple Health and shows how much time you spent in each of five heart-rate
training zones.

## Features

- **Day / Week views** — a per-zone bar chart of time spent in each zone.
- **History view** — a scrollable weekly stacked-bar chart (Swift Charts) showing
  zone trends across the past year.
- **Per-zone filter** — show or hide any zone (at least one always visible) to
  focus on what matters.

Zones are computed from raw heart-rate samples using the Heart Rate Reserve
(Karvonen) method, with your max HR estimated from age and resting HR read from
Health.

## Requirements

- iOS 26+
- Swift 6.3 / SwiftUI
- A device with Apple Health workout + heart-rate data (HealthKit is unavailable
  in the Simulator)

No third-party dependencies.

## Architecture

See [`docs/architecture.md`](docs/architecture.md) for a walkthrough of the file
layout, the HealthKit data pipeline, and the app's design.

## Privacy

ZoneScope is read-only and fully on-device: it requests Health **read** access
only, writes nothing back, and has no network, accounts, or analytics.
