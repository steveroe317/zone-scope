## Summary

ZoneScope does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything the app
reads stays on your device.

## What ZoneScope reads

With your permission, ZoneScope reads the following from Apple Health:

- **Workouts** — the start and end times of your recorded workouts
- **Heart rate** — heart-rate samples recorded during those workouts
- **Resting heart rate** — your most recent resting heart-rate reading
- **Date of birth** — used only to estimate your maximum heart rate

ZoneScope requests **read-only** access. It never writes anything back to Apple Health.

## How that data is used

Your heart-rate samples are converted into time spent in each of five training zones and
displayed as charts. This calculation happens entirely on your device.

## What is stored

ZoneScope stores two things locally on your device:

1. **A derived workout cache**, so the app doesn't have to re-read your full workout
   history every time it launches. It is written with complete file protection and is
   excluded from iCloud backup, so it never leaves your device.
2. **Two display preferences** — which training zones you have chosen to show, and the
   granularity selected in the Trends view.

Deleting the app removes both.

## What is not done

ZoneScope has no network layer. Specifically, it does not:

- Send your health data, or any other data, off your device
- Include analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking of any kind
- Use third-party SDKs or services
- Require or offer an account
- Store health data in iCloud

## Your control

You can review or revoke ZoneScope's access to your health data at any time in the Health
app, under **Sharing → Apps**, or in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health**. Revoking
access does not delete data from Apple Health; it only stops ZoneScope from reading it.

## Children

ZoneScope is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data from children.
